# VPN Connection Troubleshooting

If you are unable to connect to the company VPN:

1. Verify that your internet connection is active.
2. Restart the VPN client.
3. Ensure your VPN credentials are correct.
4. Clear any saved VPN sessions.
5. Update the VPN client to the latest version.
6. Try reconnecting.

If the VPN still fails, raise a Network Support ticket.
